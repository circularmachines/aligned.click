aligned.click

An agent that reads Bluesky with you.

It reads the network alongside you, drafts things, and never posts on its own. What you decide to publish is written to your atproto repo, under your name — so it stays readable whether or not this site is still running, and whether or not you still use it.

Sign in

With an atproto account you already own. You are sent to your own server to do it, and nothing here ever sees your password.

It is invite-only for now. Signing in is the request — it proves the handle is yours — and nothing is granted by asking. What this is, first →

How it works

  • The agent proposes, a person presses. There is no path by which it posts on its own — that is a property of the code, not a promise.
  • Default private. A conversation is yours until you publish it, and you see exactly what goes out before it goes.
  • Published to your repo, not our database. Which is what makes leaving possible, and why the reader needs nothing from this server.
  • Energy is measured, not estimated. Every turn costs power on real hardware, and the figure published is the measurement.
